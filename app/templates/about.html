About Brantford Bathroom Finder

A community-powered map of public washrooms in Brantford, Ontario — with exact indoor locations so you're never wandering a building when nature calls.

How it works
  • Our map shows verified public washrooms across Brantford.
  • Each listing includes where the bathroom is located inside the building — not just an address.
  • Community members can rate bathrooms on cleanliness, toilet paper, and soap.
  • Anyone can suggest a missing bathroom for review.
Data Sources

Data is sourced from OpenStreetMap contributors and local community members. Indoor location data is curated manually.

Privacy

No account or personal information is required. Ratings are associated with an anonymous browser session only.
